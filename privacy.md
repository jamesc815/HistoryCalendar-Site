History Calendar is a utility that store event by day on your iPhone. It is built to keep your data on your device. This policy explains what the app handles and the only circumstances in which anything leaves your phone.

* History Calendar does not sell your personal information, and collects nothing automatically or in the background.
* There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising.
* Information leaves your device when you export your events.

<h3>What the app stores</h3>
History Calendar stores the events as you create them in the app's own storage.

<h2>Contacting support</h2>
You can contact support using email listed below. 

##Data sharing
Aside from the cases described above, the developer does not receive, store, share, or sell any of your data. History Calendar contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking software of any kind.

##Data retention and deletion
Because your event data is stored only on your device, you are in full control of it: deleting the app removes all of it, and the developer holds no copy and therefore cannot access or delete it on your behalf. 

##Children's privacy
History Calendar is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.
